Biography

I hold a Ph.D. from The University of Hong Kong and an M.S. degree in Information System from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Prior to joining HKU, I served as a FinTech Engineer at the Hong Kong Applied Science and Technology Research Institute for three years, where I gained hands-on experience in financial technology applications and blockchain development.
